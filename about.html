About Me

My name is Hiya Bhatia and I am an 18 year old photographer fascinated by the nature. Through my lens, I try to capture the relationship between people, landscapes, and the small details that often go unnoticed.

My photography explores the beauty of the natural world, and the nuances of everyday life that the average person ignores.
